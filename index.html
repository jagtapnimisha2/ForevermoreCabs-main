available car types

Electric Vehicles (EVs):

A cab services offer electric vehicles as part of their fleet to provide a sustainable and environmentally friendly transportation option. EVs contribute to reducing emissions and promoting green mobility..
The cabin of an electric vehicle often features a user-friendly interface that provides information about the battery status, range, and charging options. Some EVs also come with advanced infotainment systems and connectivity features. EVs may utilize energy-efficient LED lighting in the cabin, contributing to overall energy savings and enhancing the vehicle's interior aesthetics.

Hybrid Cars:

Hybrid vehicles combine traditional internal combustion engines with electric power. They offer improved fuel efficiency and reduced environmental impact compared to conventional vehicles.
Hybrid cars often have different driving modes, such as electric-only mode, hybrid mode, and sometimes a sport mode. The cabin may feature displays indicating the current power mode and providing information about the energy flow between the engine and electric motor. Hybrid models often prioritize interior comfort and space, similar to traditional vehicles. The hybrid system components are designed to take up minimal space, allowing for a comfortable and practical cabin layout.

Luxury or Executive Cars:

Luxury or executive cars cater to passengers who prefer a higher level of comfort, style, and features. These vehicles may include premium sedans or luxury SUVs with enhanced interiors and amenities.
The seats in luxury cars are typically designed for optimal comfort and support. They may include features like heating, ventilation, and massage functions. Additionally, ergonomic design ensures a pleasant driving experience over long distances. High-end audio systems from renowned brands are a common feature in luxury cars. These systems are designed to deliver exceptional sound quality and may include advanced speaker configurations, surround sound, and noise cancellation technologies.
